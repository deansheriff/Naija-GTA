A few things before you step in.

Desktop: WASD or arrows to walk, click the street to move, E nearby to interact. Tab and Enter navigate menus. On phones, use Explore and venue buttons; portrait follows your character. Tap Amaka or Yemi to walk over and talk, even during a pavement break. Take Amaka’s lunch favor for a one-time 30 StreetCoin thank-you. The park crew remembers introductions and requests for space across saves. Choose Park crew or tap a crew member to talk. Traffic yields when you are ahead in its lane. Click travel routes around solid obstacles; shop roofs reveal the room when you enter. Choose Ride between districts to board a danfo for Market Road or Palm Junction, with an elevated side camera following the bus.

Self-hosted builds connect to the game server automatically. Open Settings → Cloud save to keep your save access code or connect another device. Keep that code private: it grants access to your character, and lost codes cannot be recovered yet. Graphics settings stay on each device.

Offline previews save locally. Cloud saves start separately; editable local balances are never imported. Activities and rewards require a connection when using a cloud save. A pending action can be retried safely.

Open Explore → Up NEPA! · Story mode for Owambe, Owambe: meet Tunde, Ngozi and KC at a wedding, complete labelled tasks and keep a checkpoint after every step. The opening campaign slice is playable; later acts are in production. Family Meeting becomes available after the introduction. Continue into Baba God Dey to drive the bus through three short road legs: accelerate, brake in the marked lower-lane bays, or use Guided approach.

This is an early life-game build. Gig drafts and practice polls are private to your save. Shared marketplace, multiplayer, live elections, Jev, news, payments and airtime/data rewards are not available.
