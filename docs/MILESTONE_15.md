# 0.16.0 — Four Percent

After Ajo Day, **Continue · Four Percent (KC)** opens eleven checkpoints. KC obtains permission for a private practice shot, frames three subjects, follows one of three charging leads, compares the cropped edit with the wider original, preserves a private copy and chooses whether to disclose his earlier omission now or leave that conversation unresolved. Both disclosure choices retain the source and all main missions; later authored repair opportunities remain planned.

## Story camera and battery

The illustrated framing board uses **Pan left/right**, **Wide/Detail**, **Save take** and **Guided full frame**. Include all three numbered subjects in the centred wide frame. Only an attempted saved take spends the displayed 1% story battery. Reading, panning, summaries, pause, reload and time closed cost nothing. A bad crop preserves the task with a hint. At zero, **Borrow enough charge** restores four points for free; original footage/evidence is never removed. A completed take cannot debit twice. The assisted take has the same cost and progression as manual framing. Keyboard: Left/Right pans, Up selects Wide, Down selects Detail, R saves, G assists and C borrows charge when empty; Tab/Enter remain normal menu navigation.

This is a static illustrated composition/review task, not device-camera access, a recording upload or a new video asset. The scene uses the existing original KC pose, code-native room/charging props and numbered reconstructions of the context. Readable subject/status labels remain outside the view. Full directional protagonist animation and richer task animation remain planned. Moving the composition updates the existing view; success replaces finished controls with the narrative decision.

The kiosk, empty power-bank and community-bench leads each have their own saved response, then rejoin at a borrowed cable and agreed turn. The original-review task retains MC, service-exit sign and the helping action together. It does not establish the entire bag sequence; testimony/timing still need corroboration. `A13-disclose` explicitly saves `admit` or `defer` for later dialogue. Chapter completion grants two KC mastery and `original_clip` once, with no StreetCoins. Street needs/jobs stay frozen while the story is active.

## Authority, saves and review

`campaign_phone` sends only expected stage and a listed control. The server owns charge, pan, zoom, take counts and saved-frame status. Unknown controls, skipped tasks and forged battery/ready payloads are rejected. Exact concurrent retries reuse the receipt, preventing duplicate battery use/recovery. Clock changes never consume battery. A depleted phone survives restart/recovery and remains playable through the free charger.

Campaign JSON advances to **version 4**, adding phone state. Version-1 wedding, version-2 road and version-3 Ajo records migrate without replay or losing choices/evidence/mastery. Root character save version 1 and PostgreSQL migration 3 remain unchanged. Future campaigns/unknown missions remain protected. Keep the production volume/password and use the new browser/APK.

Canonical `server/src/kc-intro.json` is copied into the game. The owner queue contains **108** current units, with all framing subject labels and charging-route text/summaries included in exact-content hashes. Existing unchanged units retain review hashes. Actual admin cultural sign-off remains pending.
