# Guided danfo transport and seeded districts — 0.6.0

Choose **Ride between districts** in the sidebar or Explore menu. The player walks to the current stop before boarding. The bus pulls away, cruises through reproducible street blocks, then parks at Market Road, Palm Junction or Home street. Arrival puts the character on clear pavement in a walkable destination district. Starter activities and their named NPCs remain on Home street; choosing a starter venue from another district queues the return ride and then the final walk.

The camera stays above and to the side, following the bus horizontally with a modest look-ahead. Portrait uses the same view and keeps the journey controls below the vehicle. Walking HUD panels are hidden during the ride. **Skip to arrival** retains the final pull-in; **Return to starting stop** or Escape cancels safely. Bus movement and permanent tire-contact patches work even on Low with ambient effects disabled.

## World generation and streaming

`world/world_plan.gd` fixes a common world seed, version 2 and the three destination profiles for browser/Android builds. `DistrictLayout` preserves the authored home block and creates seeded destination/transit blocks. Building order and lot positions, tree/furniture placement and ground colors vary within reserved lots. A northbound T junction uses the clear gap between building lots and joins the through-road. Doorways, counters, road widths, arrival points and continuous pedestrian corridors have explicit clearance rules. Generation never depends on the current time or external AI/news.

`core/journey.gd` owns the departure/cruise/arrival state machine. `world/journey_view.gd` requests at most three nearby scene chunks. Incoming chunks load before entering the camera window; obsolete chunks queue for deletion behind it. A continuation block fills the arrival edge, and wide-window framing is limited to the available scenery. Transient deletion can overlap for one frame; active chunk count stays bounded. Transit blocks omit navigation-grid construction, reuse the existing art and retain local pedestrians, dogs, birds and opposing traffic. Transit cars/pedestrians cross tile seams without recycling under the moving camera; they leave view naturally before their old chunk unloads. Adaptive quality changes also update already loaded chunks. The ride bus uses the clear forward lane. Adjacent transit road modules share a flat lane profile so their seams stay connected.

The kit is deliberately bounded: three selectable districts and generated transit blocks, not an unrestricted city. Secondary storefronts are scenery/interior visits; they do not clone Amaka's greeting or starter-gig rewards. No new raster assets were generated. Existing painted modules are reused, with native road/junction geometry.

## Pedestrians, dogs and game state

Passing NPCs preserve forward movement and facing. Where clear pavement exists, they gradually step around a nearby player and return to their corridor. A narrow doorway is not used as a temporary detour into an obstacle. The older pedestrian's south route now stays within the intended pavement band. Dogs reject steps into main traffic lanes or solid scenery and choose another pavement waypoint.

Transport is cosmetic local session state: no fares, currency awards, remote mission advancement or shared multiplayer vehicles. Existing starter activities still use the same local/server rules and cloud saves remain compatible. Closing/reopening starts on Home street; this version does not save/resume a remote location. A resumed tab advances only a capped movement step and cannot finish a ride using offline time. Future persistent location or fares require explicit authoritative server rules.

## Validation and delivery

The build runs 28 simulation checks, 18 street checks, 16 actor/nature checks, 22 district checks, 39 new world/travel checks and actual-scene layout/tire/recycled-gait checks: 123 named checks plus scene layout checks. New regressions validate the home block, both destinations, all real transit seeds and 64 further seeds for reachability, entrance clearance, road connectivity and pedestrian corridors. They exercise reproducibility/variation, all playable dog routes, pedestrian personal space, non-recycling transit pedestrians/cars/birds, ride phases, frame-rate independence, capped resumed frames, skip/arrival-once behavior, actual boarding, bounded scene cleanup, omitted transit navigation grids, Low tire grounding, adaptive quality changes, elevated portrait/wide-window framing, safe cancellation, completed return-to-venue travel and unchanged wallets.

Browser and native Android exports use 0.6.0, Android version code 7. The committed `web/dist` is what Coolify serves; redeploy `main` using the existing Compose app with its database/password retained. No server schema change is required. Native debug export/signing is separate from physical-device HTTPS, input, frame-time, memory and thermal validation, which remain pending.

The final browser build was reviewed in motion at desktop size and in 390×844 portrait. Both destination districts were reached; choosing Yemi's workshop from Palm Junction completed the return ride and final doorway walk. The portrait departure frame shows the entire moving bus and both ride controls without the walking HUD obscuring them. Console checks reported no errors or warnings.
