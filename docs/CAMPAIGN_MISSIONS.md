# Up NEPA! — mission production list

Design draft for the full campaign, following the [story bible](STORY_BIBLE_UP_NEPA.md). Continuous implementation is authorised in [PRODUCTION_PLAN.md](PRODUCTION_PLAN.md); cultural/native-speaker review is still pending. Mission IDs are stable save keys. Completion gives campaign mastery/unlocks only, never sandbox StreetCoins. Every stage autosaves after a validated action; retry restores the current task, not the entire act. No critical clue is permanently missable.

## Route and evidence spine

P00 → A11 → A12 → A13 → A14 → A21 → A22 → A23 → A24 → A25 → A26 → A27 → A31 → A32 → A33. Solo tasks use the named lead, while ensemble tasks explicitly stage switches. This authored route keeps introductions and reveals legible; completed chapters later support isolated replay without changing the original ending record. Side jobs never gate a main mission.

Nneka moves the Chief's bag during the wedding blackout to protect a folder of unpaid catering receipts that was put inside it. She expects to hand it to Ngozi, boards Tunde's bus in the confusion, then leaves it beneath a seat while taking an injured colleague to the caterers' room. She returns looking for it, finds the bus gone, and leaves a message. Her testimony, time-stamped service slip and the original footage let the leads establish this sequence. She is an adult worker with choices, pay owed and a life beyond the mystery.

Core evidence: `wedding_clip` (KC), `bag_record` (Tunde), `catering_invoice` (Ngozi), `nneka_account`, `service_slip`, `diary`, `note_discrepancy`, `original_clip`, `witness_consent`. The journal separates an observation, an allegation and corroboration. Returning the bag does not erase copied receipts or testimony. Missing optional investigation is recoverable through a clearly marked evidence review before the finale.

## Prologue — Owambe, Owambe

**P00 · all three · The wedding that needs an extension cable.** Objective sequence: Tunde checks the guest colour against an invitation and directs guests through a clear aisle; Ngozi matches three numbered food tickets to trays without treating queue-jumpers as enemies; KC frames the MC, consenting dancers and the exit sign, then saves the original clip. Money spraying uses stage-prop tokens: aim at a collection tray with a no-pressure practice/retry and no currency purchase. The MC introduces the groom's mother for a third entrance; Tunde's bus becomes the unofficial cloakroom. Blackout is a gentle fade with a reduced-effects alternative. Nneka carries the bag past the service exit; only a readable silhouette and time cue are shown. Light returns; title. Reward: 1 mastery for each lead, `wedding_clip`, all three introductions and A11. Checkpoints after each perspective and immediately before blackout. Failure: mismatched tray/colour/frame prompts a hint and retry without deduction. Alternative input: buttons and text targets; no precision or rapid input required.

## Endings, explicit conditions and epilogues

- **Honest:** always available at A33 after recording the bag; choose accountable return through the fictional community trust. Community repairs are shown with a posted project ledger. Tunde still runs a rattling bus; Ngozi gets catering work paid properly; KC must build an audience honestly. Earn ending record `honest`.
- **Hustle:** always available; choose to keep the property despite an explicit warning about trust. Story props become disputed comforts, no sandbox payout. Tunde's bigger sign hides the same engine, Ngozi withdraws her guarantee, KC's audience wants the next scandal. Record `hustle`.
- **Japa:** complete or recover the fictional fellowship callback and choose accountable property disposition plus KC's departure. KC earns a farewell and maintains contact; Ngozi and Tunde choose their own continuing projects. Record `japa`; the journal describes the callback route before selection.
- **Receipts:** preserve the diary, compare old-note discrepancy, retain the original clip and verify Nneka's account/consent or anonymised service record. Explicit “Follow the receipts” option appears once those visible journal conditions are satisfied. Reveal the diary's pattern of incompatible promises; community action matters more than the apparent cash. Record `receipts`.

Endings are player choices rather than arbitrary highest-score selection. A32 remains replayable until the finale commitment. After an ending, a separate chapter-replay copy allows other choices without overwriting the original run, farming unlocks or requiring a destructive reset.

## Side activities and recovery

Tunde: **Precious Cargo** (deliver wedding chairs without blocking doorways), **Last Bus** (listen to a tired commuter), **Express Dreams** (plan a modest fleet budget). Ngozi: **Lunch Before Drama** (match three food orders), **The Missing 200** (reconcile a bookkeeping error and choose forgiveness), **A Name on the Door** (plan a catering sign). KC: **One Clean Take** (frame three consenting subjects), **Buffering Courage** (correct a wrong caption), **Borrowed Cable** (return a charger and acknowledge its owner). Each gives one optional mastery once, a dialogue/cosmetic unlock and a repeatable practice version with no reward farming. No side objective requires a real payment, real account or outside API.

Recovery is always free: safe-bay pause lowers Tunde's fictional stress, canteen break restores Ngozi's confidence, borrowed charger restores KC's battery. Missions can be paused/closed with their checkpoint intact. No offline battery drain, silent evidence loss, random tow bills or forced grind.
