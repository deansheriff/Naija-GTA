**Summary:** Tunde resolved a fare dispute and recorded a bag under his bus seat. The tag links it to the wedding. He asks Ngozi for help. Reward/evidence and A12 checkpoint save atomically.

**Canteen, late afternoon.** Generator noise is indicated by subtitle, never so loud dialogue becomes inaccessible. Tunde: “The bag entered my bus without paying.” KC: “That line needs a camera.” Ngozi: “It needs a witness.” Player stages each lead at the table, compares `bag_record`, `catering_invoice`, `original_clip`, then moves outside the noisy doorway. Nneka's account deserves corroboration and privacy. Switching and side work unlock; A21 begins when the player is ready. Checkpoint and awards save once.
